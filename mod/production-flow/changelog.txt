Version: 0.1.1
Date: 2026-10-24
  Features:
    - Built-in question-mark help button with controls, calculation and stock explanations.
    - History warm-up indicator and explicit Update list action for held Balance rows.
    - Producing recipe names ranked by contribution, including casting and condensation.
  Changes:
    - Rename Live controls to Recent and Auto-update chart to distinguish their purpose.
    - Use cautious activity/capacity status wording and mark estimated empty times.
    - Keep diagnostic exports and renderer comparisons out of normal menus.
    - Disable automatic diagnostic exports by default for new installations.
  Bugfixes:
    - Correct negative logistic stock by adding back pending robot pickup reservations.
    - Reset the old stock baseline on upgrade to avoid an artificial stock trend.
    - Identify the selected material in Uses headings and reserve space for both lines.
