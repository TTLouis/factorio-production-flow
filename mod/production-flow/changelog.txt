Version: 0.4.11
Date: 2026-11-21
  Features:
    - Added the Cells tab for grouping factory machines into named production cells (including nested subdivisions) with Flow, Map, Train stops and Train History sub-tabs.
    - Track measured per-cell throughput alongside full-speed Capacity (including estimated burner fuel demand), order cells in production stages in the All view, preserve cell membership across machine upgrades/rebuilds and follow Space Exploration spaceship clones.
    - Single-selection cell setup automatically links selected train stops and storage buffers, suggests default inputs and outputs from recipe topology and boundary belts/pipes/chests (keeping internal intermediates excluded by default), and auto-selects the primary recipe or product icon and name.
    - Link train stops and buffer chests/tanks to cells to monitor live scheduled/headed/stopped train counts (with train-limit capacity indicators), live train preview with zoom and remote-view jump, and recorded cargo exchange history.
  Gui:
    - Highlight cell machines, linked train stops and associated storage buffers in both world and zoomed-out map/chart view, and outline all registered cells on the current surface while holding a cell selection tool.
    - Allow choosing deduplicated unique recipe icons alongside items and fluids as a cell's display icon, and use the Production Flow icon on the cell selection tool and shortcut.
    - Give Full Sankey more room with tighter column spacing and a single toolbar row when no extra controls are needed.
    - Add a clickable overview with all routed flows. Move grouping controls into Selected material and add shortcuts to Focus and Balance.
    - Show recipe rates and observed machine icons on material hover and in the Selected material Breakdown. Open Machine status from the map icon beside Breakdown. List active recipes hidden from the chart in a separate section.
    - Zoom the whole Full Sankey chart in 10-percentage-point steps. Fit rounds down to the largest step that fits both axes.
  Bugfixes:
    - Use rounded bends on steep flows and close gaps at ribbon joins, including at fractional UI scales.
    - Preserve dropdowns, sidebar controls and unfinished group names during live and manual graph refreshes.

Version: 0.3.10
Date: 2026-11-19
  Features:
    - Added optional Space Exploration support, with Transfers views for observed cargo rocket launches and delivery cannon shipments.
    - Measure Space Exploration receipts when a cargo rocket lands on its landing pad, including cargo lost in flight, and when a delivery chest catches a cannon package. Off-pad landings and spilled packages stay unknown and are marked as unavailable in History charts instead of drawn as full deliveries. Balance History and chest-stock estimates include measured Space Exploration receipts.
    - Browse Space Exploration shipment History by time or event, with filters and shortcuts to recorded launch pads, landing pads, delivery cannons and receiver chests.
    - Focus and Full Sankey show observed exports to other surfaces as transport nodes. Click one to open its transfer history.
  Gui:
    - The Space Exploration Flow card shows the selected surface's details from Space Exploration: zone type, its moon, planet and star, primary resource, environment, threat, robot interference, solar, hazards, day/night cycle, radius and its top five resources with size and richness.
    - Switch Space Exploration Flow between Transport and Items. Grouped by item, each side lists its eight most transported items, and an item keeps the same colour on both sides and in every card.
    - Expanded shipment History rows list each item's sent, lost and received amounts in one table. Delivery cannon shots that no chest caught are marked on the shipment row.
    - Estimate remaining Space Exploration rocket cargo from launch-time safety research and the route when available. The chart explains when it shows a dispatch quantity and why a receipt is unknown.
    - Space Age platform cards list Loaded, Made aboard, Unloaded and Kept aboard in flow order.
