# Beta screenshots

Fresh native captures from Production Flow 0.1.0 on Factorio 2.1.23, Space Age.

Charts default to individual item/fluid names. Focus, Full Sankey and grouping need more polish.

- [Water inputs and uses](01-balance-water.png): Steam condensation and water consumption per consumer.
- [Organized item groups](02-balance-groups.png): Shaded headers, row and column dividers, and vertical-only scrolling.
- [Details within each row](03-balance-details.png): Utilisation, net percentage and stock trend remain paired with their values.
- [Full Sankey on Nauvis](04-full-nauvis.png): An overview of measured production across the selected surface.
- [Fluid processing on Vulcanus](05-vulcanus-oil.png): Condensation, liquefaction and connected fluid recipes.
- [A pinned petroleum chain](06-petroleum-pinned.png): Highlight connected flows and dim unrelated branches.
- [Focus on water](07-focus-condensation.png): Needs more polish: upstream and downstream recipe connections.
- [Iron plate consumption](08-balance-iron-usage.png): Balance: Uses show consumption of iron plates per second.
- [Steam inputs and uses](09-balance-steam-usage.png): Balance: steam consumed by condensation and liquefaction.
- [Local platform overview](10-space-platform.png): Unfinished grouping: broad categories are optional; individual item/fluid names are the default.
- [Copper chain with plain material names](11-copper-chain-plain.png): Full Sankey needs more polish; individual Calcite, Copper ore, Molten copper and Copper plate names are shown.
