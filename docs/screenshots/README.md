# Beta screenshots

Native Production Flow 0.1.1 captures on Factorio 2.1.23, Space Age.

Charts default to individual item/fluid names. Focus, Full Sankey and grouping need more polish.

- [Water inputs and uses](01-balance-water.png): Steam condensation, selected-water consumption rates and the built-in question-mark help button.
- [Organized item groups](02-balance-groups.png): Shaded group headings, row and column dividers, vertical-only scrolling and an explicit Update list action.
- [Details within each row](03-balance-details.png): Utilisation, net percentage and stock trend remain paired with their values.
- [Full Sankey on Nauvis](04-full-nauvis.png): Needs more polish. Optional grouped view of measured production on the selected surface.
- [Fluid processing on Vulcanus](05-vulcanus-oil.png): Full Sankey needs more polish. Condensation, liquefaction and connected fluid recipes.
- [A pinned petroleum chain](06-petroleum-pinned.png): Full Sankey needs more polish. Highlight connected flows and dim unrelated branches.
- [Focus on water](07-focus-condensation.png): Focus needs more polish. Upstream and downstream recipe connections.
- [Iron plate consumption](08-balance-iron-usage.png): Uses show iron plates consumed per second, not the consumer product output.
- [Steam inputs and uses](09-balance-steam-usage.png): Steam consumed by condensation and liquefaction, with the material named in the Uses heading.
- [Local platform overview](10-space-platform.png): Full Sankey and grouping need more polish. Grouping is optional; individual item/fluid names are the default.
- [Copper chain with plain material names](11-copper-chain-plain.png): Full Sankey needs more polish. Calcite, Copper ore, Molten copper and Copper plate are named individually.
- [Copper casting in Balance](12-balance-copper-casting.png): Plain Copper plate name with its recorded Casting copper recipe and copper consumption per consumer.
- [Corrected stored logistic stock](13-balance-stored-stock.png): Nauvis steel stock is 101 stored items; the old -3 availability count subtracted 104 pending pickups.
- [In-game help: Start here](14-help-basics.png): Controls, stable list updates, material and recipe names, and unfinished features.
- [In-game help: Numbers & stock](15-help-stock.png): History, measured rates, selected-material consumption, stored stock and estimates.
- [In-game help: Alerts & charts](16-help-alerts.png): Heuristic alerts, estimated utilisation, recipe links, widths, colors and scope.
- [Fresh-install history warm-up](17-history-warmup.png): No prior history is invented. History collected shows the available time toward the selected 600 seconds.
- [Recent averaging period](18-recent-period.png): Recent (60 s) is separate from the Auto-update chart switch; the default period remains 10 minutes.
